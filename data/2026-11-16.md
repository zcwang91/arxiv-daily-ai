Method: 以交替扭转三层石墨烯（ATTG）莫尔平带超导体为平台，测量超导随填充、温度和磁场的演化；利用面外与面内磁场驱动相变，提取临界电阻、有限温标度指数zν_B；并测量玻色绝缘体的输运特征（电阻量子化、零偏压峰、霍尔电阻、对数温依赖）以及大偏压下的动力学驱动行为。

Result: 超导在莫尔平带半填充附近最优，高温下转为密度波式电阻峰，配对温度尺度约6 K、凝聚温度尺度约1 K；观察到玻色型超导量子相变，普适临界电阻R_c≈0.62R_Q（R_Q=h/4e²），zν_B≈2.3；玻色绝缘体电阻近量子化至R_Q并在低温高场饱和，伴随零偏压峰、近零霍尔电阻及高温对数依赖；超导体与玻色绝缘体在大偏压下均可被动态驱动至同一临界量子涨落区。

Conclusion: 莫尔平带可作为研究由量子涨落主导的非常规超导与玻色量子临界性的干净平台，量子相位涨落是统一超导与玻色绝缘量子临界性的组织原则。

Abstract: Quantum fluctuations play a central role in superconductors by mediating the interactions that promote electron pairing and controlling the phase coherence required for macroscopic condensation. Thus far, understanding the interplay between quantum fluctuations and superconductivity remains challenging in unconventional superconductors. Here, we establish quantum phase fluctuations as the organizing principle linking superconductivity and Bose-insulating quantum criticality in a moiré flatband superconductor of alternating twisted trilayer graphene (ATTG). Superconductivity is optimal near half-filling of the moiré flatband and gives way to a density-wave-like resistance peak at high temperatures, in which we observe distinct temperature scales for the pairing (~6 K) and the condensation (~1 K). We observe compelling evidence of a bosonic superconducting quantum phase transition, driven by both out-of-plane and in-plane magnetic fields, with a universal critical resistivity of $R_c =~0.62R_Q$ and finite-temperature scaling of $zν_B = 2.3$, where $R_Q = h/4e^2$ is the quantum resistance for bosons. Strikingly, the observed bosonic insulator shows a nearly quantized resistivity of $R_Q$ that saturates at low temperatures and high magnetic fields, together with a zero-bias peak, a nearly zero Hall resistance, and a logarithmic dependence at higher temperatures. Both the superconductor and the bosonic insulator could be driven into the same critical quantum fluctuation regime dynamically under a large bias. Our results establish moiré flatband as a clean platform for studying unconventional superconductivity and bosonic quantum criticality governed by quantum fluctuations.
